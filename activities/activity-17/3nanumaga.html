The Aotearoa Canoe

Tītī Tōrea is a really cool and exciting traditional game played by the Māori people of Aotearoa (New Zealand). Players sit or stand in groups and pass short sticks to each other in rhythm while chanting or singing. The game requires fast reflexes, teamwork, timing, and concentration.
