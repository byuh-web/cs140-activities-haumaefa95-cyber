Don't give up on me

He instituted the sacrament as a reminder of His great atoning sacrifice.
He was arrested and condemned on spurious charges,
convicted to satisfy a mob,
and sentenced to die on Calvary’s cross.
He gave His life to atone for the sins of all mankind.
His was a great vicarious gift in behalf of all who would ever live upon the earth.

We solemnly testify that His life,
which is central to all human history,
neither began in Bethlehem nor concluded on Calvary.
He was the Firstborn of the Father,
the Only Begotten Son in the flesh, the Redeemer of the world.
