Welcome to Nanumea Island

Nanumea is the northernmost island of Tuvalu and holds a special place in the nation’s history and identity. Known for its strong sense of tradition and pride, Nanumea is often considered one of the cultural heartlands of Tuvalu, where customs, language, and community values remain deeply rooted in everyday life. The island is surrounded by a beautiful lagoon and rich marine life, which support fishing as a main source of livelihood. Historically, Nanumea played an important role during World War II, when it was used as a base by Allied forces, leaving behind stories and remnants that still connect the island to global history. Despite its isolation, Nanumea continues to thrive through the resilience of its people, who maintain a close-knit community and a deep respect for their land, sea, and cultural heritage.
