Tahiti Canoe

At the PCC, visitors can see or ride in Tahitian-style outrigger canoes along the lagoon while learning how Polynesian ancestors explored and settled islands across the Pacific. The canoe symbolizes adventure, teamwork, and survival, showing how important the ocean was to Polynesian life and culture.
