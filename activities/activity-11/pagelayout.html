Ankara Messi

2006 World Cup 2010 World Cup 2014 World Cup 2018 World Cup 2022 World Cup

Lionel Messi’s World Cup story is one of persistence, heartbreak, and ultimate triumph. Across five tournaments (2006–2022), Messi transformed from a teenage prodigy into a global icon who finally lifted football’s most coveted trophy.

🇩🇪 2006 – The Young Prodigy

At just 18 years old, Messi played his first World Cup in Germany. He scored his first World Cup goal against Serbia and Montenegro, becoming one of Argentina’s youngest-ever scorers in the tournament. Argentina were eliminated in the quarterfinals.

🇿🇦 2010 – High Expectations

In South Africa, Messi wore the famous No. 10 shirt. Despite strong performances, he did not score in the tournament. Argentina were knocked out in the quarterfinals by Germany, leading to heavy criticism and pressure back home.

🇧🇷 2014 – So Close to Glory

This was Messi’s breakthrough World Cup as a leader. He scored 4 goals and carried Argentina to the final against Germany. Argentina lost 1–0 in extra time, but Messi won the Golden Ball as the tournament’s best player. It was a painful near-miss that defined his international career for years.

🇷🇺 2018 – A Difficult Campaign

The 2018 tournament in Russia was turbulent for Argentina. Messi scored a memorable goal against Nigeria in the group stage, but the team was eliminated in the Round of 16 by France. Questions about his international legacy intensified.

In Qatar, Messi delivered a legendary performance:

  • goals and 3 assists
  • Scored in the group stage, knockout rounds, semifinal, and final
  • Led Argentina to an epic final against France

The final ended 3–3 after extra time, and Argentina won on penalties. Messi scored twice in the final and converted his penalty in the shootout. He was awarded the Golden Ball again, becoming the first player to win it twice.

At last, Messi lifted the World Cup trophy — the one title that had eluded him and the achievement that completed his legacy.
